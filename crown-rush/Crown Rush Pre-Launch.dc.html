Event Rules
✕
1. Event period
One calendar month per season. This season: 12/01 00:00:00 – 12/31 23:59:59 (GMT+8).
2. Bets & EXP
EXP = valid bets ÷ the conversion rate of each game category. Accrued in real time, subject to backend configuration.
Slots₱1 = 1 EXP
Live Casino₱20 = 1 EXP
eBingo₱5 = 1 EXP
Fishing₱4 = 1 EXP
Poker₱10 = 1 EXP
Hedged bets, cancelled bets and bets on non-participating games do not earn EXP.
3. Cash rewards
Claim manually on this page once a stage is cleared. Claim-all is supported and cash is credited straight to your account.
4. Physical prizes
Available once you clear the stage within quota; submit shipping details and have stock confirmed. The claim button is disabled at zero stock — first come, first served.
5. Risk control
Hedging, arbitrage or abnormal betting may lead to disqualification and reward recovery.
